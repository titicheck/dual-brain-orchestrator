## Problem & SSOT Boundary Rationale

1. **Downstream coupling from duplicated internal mechanics:** The previous copyable `## Orchestrator` adoption snippets in `README.md` and `README.zh-CN.md` duplicated internal `SKILL.md` operational mechanics (`bound_subagent_id` three-way routing, pre-dispatch `invoke_subagent`/`send_message` verification, post-compaction `check-origin` fail-closed handling, and `--tier` CLI flag details). Every internal refinement in `SKILL.md` therefore risked drifting against downstream `AGENTS.md` / `GEMINI.md` files and shared rule libraries (`engineering-kit`), violating SSOT.
2. **Whitelist-first pre-load boundary vs. SSOT split:** The external adoption snippet belongs strictly to the pre-load boundary that must fire *before* `SKILL.md` is read:
   - **External adoption snippet (whitelist-first + 2 hard defers):** (a) explicit whitelist activation pointing to `SKILL.md` (load only in top-level human-originated—including post-compaction—single-model sessions when planning or executing tasks that modify files, state, or external side effects, or when resuming the bound `Execution Subagent`), (b) two explicit hard conflict defers (`exit_plan_mode`/`enter_plan_mode` for Official Native Orchestrator, and `/swarm`), so child subagents (`step 0` or future worker types), pure Q&A, and zero-edit reviews are filtered out automatically without maintaining an open-ended exclusion list, and (c) the static `orchestrator-tier:` directive with its conversational non-override lock and per-project `AGENTS.md` override hint.
   - **`skills/orchestrator/SKILL.md` (100% self-contained SSOT):** Keeps every internal rule intact (`Gate 0`–`Gate 3(b)`, `Direct Answer Gate`, Three-Way `[Message]` Routing, `invoke_subagent`/`send_message` tool checks, post-compaction `check-origin`, and `--tier` resolution/precedence) so zero-config `/orchestrator` sessions, post-compaction `Trunk Session` turns, and post-compaction `<skills>` condition `(3)` wakeups (`bound_subagent_id` non-null) reload `SKILL.md` and execute identically without relying on external rule duplication.
