# Bidirectional Execution Subagent Rotation

`orchestrator` does not rotate its bound `Execution Subagent` based on unconditional child-side self-compaction (`CHECKPOINT`) or inter-dispatch wall-clock idle TTL timers. While `SPAWN_NEW` rotation remains primarily driven by `Trunk Session` boundaries (`tier != bound_tier`, trunk `checkpoint_step`, trunk rewind digest/step mismatch, or mid-flight cancel `reset` or `--force-spawn` respawn), `v1.2.0` adds deterministic, clock-free expected-cost `Execution Subagent` rotation (`0.60 * C_old > 1.55 * C_new + 15,000`, strictly gated on `last_bound_report_step > last_synced_step` and fail-open on missing/unreadable child logs, with `--subagent-transcript` adapter isolation) per [ADR-0002](../docs/adr/0002-checkpoint-bounded-living-memory-inheritance.md) and [Ticket 18](../.scratch/orchestrator-v1/issues/18-cache-cold-right-brain-rotation-and-lossless-plan-fidelity.md).

## Why this is out of scope

- **Incremental memory sync already covers uncompacted idle periods:** `RESUME_EXISTING` already exports `(last_synced_step .. max_folded_step]` delta pages of the `Living Memory Window`, so idling across the 300s KV-cache cliff does not cause memory drift on an uncompacted child.
- **Hot-path child compaction rotation causes negative ROI and high-watermark thrashing:** Direct frame-level inspection of `L0-3` child `2581ba32` (`transcript.jsonl`) proved that while immediate post-compaction steps (`335`, `648`, `1009`) had `cache_read_tokens = 0`, subsequent `< 300s` `RESUME_EXISTING` dispatches (`steps 395/396` `79.6%`, `855/856` `98.6%`, and `967/968` `99.2%`—noting `step 967` preceded `CHECKPOINT 1008`) hit hot KV cache cleanly (`~$0.03–$0.04` per step). Rotating unconditionally on child compaction destroys hot caches and—when `Trunk Session` carries a large `Living Memory Window` (`44.1 KB` median / `96.3 KB` max measured in [02-exploratory-notes-and-host-probes.md](../docs/research/02-exploratory-notes-and-host-probes.md); `~100k–140k` tokens in `L0-3`)—creates a cold-start compaction thrashing loop.
- **Disproportionate cost/complexity ratio for wall-clock idle-TTL and unconditional child-`CHECKPOINT` rotation (Pareto boundary):** Across the `14.4`-hour `L0-3` session (`$57.01` total), cold-reloading the four long-idle events (`steps 1144, 1277, 1299, 1340` at `154k`, `322k`, `344k`, `385k`) versus fresh spawns yields only a `$1.68` (`2.95%`) gross counterfactual delta (`~$1.05` prefill-only savings, or `~$1.0–$1.5` / `~2%` estimated net after file re-read overhead; `$0.16` / `0.7%` on `0f48e729`). Introducing wall-clock idle-TTL timers, timestamp non-determinism, or unconditional child-`CHECKPOINT` rotation violates the V1 Pareto boundary in [ADR-0006](../docs/adr/0006-pareto-boundary-reject-lifecycle-hooks-and-workflow-carrier.md) (whereas clock-free expected-cost `Pure q=0` rotation captures bloat savings deterministically without wall-clock coupling).

## Status Update (v1.2.0 / v1.2.1)

- Wall-clock idle-TTL rotation and unconditional child-`CHECKPOINT` rotation remain permanently out of scope.
- [Ticket 18](../.scratch/orchestrator-v1/issues/18-cache-cold-right-brain-rotation-and-lossless-plan-fidelity.md) (`v1.2.0`) closed the post-Ticket-08 re-evaluation with deterministic, clock-free expected-cost rotation (`0.60 * C_old > 1.55 * C_new + 15,000`): freshly compacted small children (`~27K`) and small-`Execution Subagent` / large-`LMW` pairs stay hot on `RESUME_EXISTING`, while high-context bloated `Execution Subagents` rotate cleanly to `SPAWN_NEW` only after returning a `<report>`. Missing/unreadable child logs or non-Antigravity layouts fail open cleanly (`RESUME_EXISTING`), preserving multi-client decoupling via `--subagent-transcript`.
